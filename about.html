Who we are

Started with one wedding. Built on referrals.

We began in 2013 with a single wedding in a Chittagobg CLUB and a promise to the family: "You'll be a guest at your own celebration." They told their friends, those friends told theirs, and more than a decade later most of our work still comes from referrals.

Today we're 80+ planners, designers, producers and hospitality specialists, with our own design studio, production warehouse and offices in Chattogram,. The promise hasn't changed.
